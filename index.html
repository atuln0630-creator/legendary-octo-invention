🩹 Basic First Aid Awareness

Minor Cuts

Tell an adult, gently clean a minor cut with clean running water and cover it with a clean dressing.

Burns

Tell an adult immediately. Cool a minor burn under cool running water and seek medical advice. Do not apply ice or home remedies.

Emergency

Get an adult and seek urgent medical help. In India, call 112 for an emergency.
